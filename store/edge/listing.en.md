Length: 96 characters.

## Description

Frunchy reads your Crunchyroll watch history in your own browser, where you are already signed in, and syncs it to your Frunchy account. Frunchy is a social stats platform for anime: once your history is in, you get anime stats you can share.

How it works
Open your Crunchyroll history page and press Scrape history. Frunchy scrolls through the page for you and keeps every episode it finds on your computer. It also uploads the history to your Frunchy account. Later scrapes stop by themselves when they reach history that is already synced.

What it never touches
Your Crunchyroll password and sign-in tokens stay in your browser. Frunchy only sends your watch history, together with the Crunchyroll profile id, to your Frunchy account, using the sync key you give it.

Getting started
1. Create a Frunchy account.
2. In your account, open "Connect extension" and copy your sync key.
3. Paste the key into the extension's settings and press Save and check.
4. Open your Crunchyroll history and press Scrape history.

Frunchy is self-hosted: if you run your own Frunchy server, enter its address in the extension's settings.

Frunchy is not affiliated with Crunchyroll or MyAnimeList.
